Privacy policy

What I collect

  • When you buy something. Payments go through Stripe. Stripe collects your name, email address, and payment details. I see your name, email, and what you bought. I never see or store your card number.
  • When you book a session. Bookings go through Google Calendar. It collects your name and email address so I can send you the details.
  • When you send me your answers. Your finished workbook and the questions for your 2027 reading come to me by email. They can include your first name, your Enneagram type, the pattern you keep repeating, and what you want from the year.
  • When you email me. I keep your email address and what you wrote so I can reply.

What stays on your device

The notes you type into your 2027 reading page or The Compass Notebook save in your own browser, on the phone or computer you write them on. They are not sent to me, and I cannot see them.

How I use it

I use your information to deliver what you bought, write your feedback or reading, schedule your session, and answer your questions. I do not sell your information, and I do not share it with anyone except the services I use to run my business.

Services that handle your information

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google runs my email and booking calendar, and supplies the fonts on this website.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Skool hosts my community, if you choose to join it.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Like most websites, the hosting and font services may record basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address and browser type.

Cookies, tracking, and Do Not Track

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and it does not follow you across other websites. Because I do not track you, the site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

How long I keep it

I keep order and email records for as long as I need them to deliver your order, answer questions about it, and keep my business records.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct, or delete the information I have about you. Email lisa@thetypedtarot.com and I will take care of it, unless I am required to keep a record.

Testimonials

I only share a testimonial with the person's permission, using their first name. If someone received my work for free or at a discount, for example as a tester, I say so beside their words.

Age

My work is for adults. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will post the new version on this page with a new date at the top.
